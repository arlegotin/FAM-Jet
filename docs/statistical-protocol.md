# Crossed inference protocol, analysis-input schema v1

Recorded 18 October 2026 before scientific calibration, pilot or protection. This implements design §10, Task19 and the dated sequencing amendment. Code acceptance, endpoint Monte Carlo precision, conditional p Monte Carlo precision, sampling calibration and design eligibility are separate decisions. **Task19 scientific calibration remains open; confirmation and replication remain closed.**

## Estimand and resampling

The input is a complete finite independent-unit × paired-training-replicate matrix. A unit is an independent synthetic history or a frozen temporal cluster. Positive effects are baseline loss minus candidate loss. Existing v2 scoring averages queries within task/history, weights tasks equally and preserves fallback losses. Analysis calls `history_effects`, `history_effects_inventory` and `aggregate_scores`; it does not train models, regenerate predictions, implement a second scorer or drop failed queries. All methods must share targets, target semantics, training statistics, query identities and the frozen scoring policy.

Within a temporal cluster, declared member histories (for example stations) are averaged equally before inference. All stations in a cluster therefore share a resample index. Every history belongs to exactly one declared unit; every unit belongs to one stratum. Cluster membership, station weighting and 28-day primary/56-day sensitivity boundaries must be frozen by the future scientific manifest. Equal member weighting is this schema's sole supported within-unit rule; changing it needs a prospective version/amendment. The resampler cannot establish that clusters are independent.

For each stratum g, independently sample Hg histories with replacement. Sample S paired training replicates with replacement **once across every stratum**, independently of each history stream. Compute the mean over the Cartesian product of repeated history and seed indices, then sum using frozen positive stratum weights wg summing to one. The standalone API defaults to equal recognized strata, never their observed frequencies; confirmation uses explicit frozen regime weights.

Production implements the identical Cartesian mean using multinomial counts:

`T*b = Σg wg Σh,s (Wbgh/Hg) dghs (Ubs/S)`.

Counts include repeated indices; query rows are never resampled as independent observations. Independent explicit repeated-index enumeration is the software oracle. A power-of-two rescaling and stratum centering precede normalized-count reductions, preventing raw count sums and constant maximum-float means from overflowing. This changes numerical reduction, not the procedure. It is not a guarantee of correctly rounded arbitrary cancellation. Nonfinite effects and genuinely nonrepresentable endpoint statistics block analysis explicitly. Existing native-metric nonrepresentability flags remain unavailable rather than being reconstructed from residuals.

The primary interval is the two-sided 95% percentile interval with explicit `numpy.quantile(method='linear')`. The secondary null centers by the single observed weighted mean; its crossed means are obtained by subtracting that mean from the same draws. The two-sided p-value is `(1 + count(abs(null_mean) >= abs(observed_mean))) / (B + 1)`, including ties. Apply nominal Holm step-down at .05 to the **frozen secondary family only**; exploratory comparisons do not enter that family. Preserve nominal point p, adjusted p, thresholds, ranks and decisions. Primary percentile exclusion and centered absolute-null rejection can differ under skewness; neither implies the other.

## Literal endpoint stopping and conditional p precision

`crossed_interval(d, strata, seed, draws, *, stratum_weights=None, p_mc_error_budget=None, p_mc_family_size=1)` requires initial `draws=2000`. Four independent batches start at 500 each, append equal independent increments and double the total through 4000, 8000, 16000, 32000, 64000 and 128000. At every stage compute pooled endpoints and four batch endpoints. Each endpoint SE is the sample SD (`ddof=1`) of its four estimates divided by sqrt(4). Both SEs must be **strictly less** than .01 times pooled width. Record every stage, batch endpoint, SE, pooled width and final B. Exact all-zero A/A alone gets `degenerate_zero` at 2000; all other zero-width cases continue through the cap and retain `cap_unmet`/`zero_width_non_aa`. Cap failure withholds strong significance claims. This noisy four-batch diagnostic is not a confidence bound or evidence of population coverage.

RNG version `crossed-v1` uses `derive_seed(root, 'crossed-v1', canonical_hash({'batch': b, 'axis': axis, 'stratum': g}))`. The shared seed axis has `stratum=None`; each history axis has its actual string stratum. Streams are NumPy PCG64 multinomial generators. Process at most 256 draws per chunk, batches in order 0–3, appending equal increments at total doubling. Record actual NumPy version, root seed, every derived axis seed, chunk and append policy, quantile method and arithmetic convention. Replay requires this algorithm, library version, ordered matrix, frozen weights and provenance. Different comparison seeds are frozen independently; paired scientific data can still make comparison outcomes dependent.

No scientific p-MC error budget has been chosen. Null budget yields `not_assessed` and withholds strong secondary rejection. If prospectively specified, conditional binomial Clopper–Pearson tail bounds spend the frozen budget across seven stages × secondary-family size × two tails, then transform each bound q to `(1+B*q)/(B+1)`. This union bound covers the endpoint-selected stage without p-driven resampling. Compare Holm decisions using lower and upper p bounds; disagreement gives `unresolved`, preserving nominal p/Holm and withholding strong rejection. Agreement gives `adequate` for this conditional diagnostic only. It does not calibrate marginal p-values. There are no extra p-driven draws.

## Single Task21 input boundary

`analyze_confirmation(manifest: Path, unblinded: Path, out: Path) -> dict` writes a **fresh JSON file**. It accepts the following v1 JSON manifest, whose own canonical hash belongs in the separate release record and scores are bound to the separately committed score-manifest hash (avoiding self-referential hashes):

| Field | Required meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `schema_version`, `scope_id` | 1; nonempty frozen scientific or explicitly software-only scope |
| `test_only`, `namespace` | Boolean; `test_only` iff true, otherwise `protected_confirmation` or `protected_replication` |
| `source_hash` | Actual all-package `source_identity()`; no exemption for later package edits |
| `score_manifest_hash`, `statistics_hash` | Frozen scorer-manifest commitment and canonical training-only statistics hash |
| `scoring_policy` | Existing complete v2 scoring policy, including expected query/task/history/replicate/regime inventory, equal task and explicit regime weights |
| `methods` | Unique complete expected methods; each belongs to a declared comparison |
| `expected_runs` | Complete method × paired-replicate list of `{run_id, method, replicate}` |
| `units` | Ordered `{unit_id, stratum, history_ids}` list, disjoint and exhaustive independently of observed output files |
| `comparisons` | Named `{pair: [baseline,candidate], role, seed}`; exactly one `primary`, frozen `secondary` family and optionally `exploratory` |
| `files` | `{role, path, sha256}` records, paths relative to manifest, with all roles `source`, `dependencies`, `data`, `config`, `weights`, `statistics`; multiple files per role allowed; exact file bytes checked |
| `release_record` | Relative path to separate administrative release evidence |
| `calibration_policy`, `calibration_evidence` | Frozen policy and retained per-cell counts/provenance, or explicit unfilled policy and empty list |
| `p_mc_error_budget` | Explicit frozen conditional budget, currently null for science |
| `design_evidence` | Required for scientific eligibility: explicit accepted `implementation`, matching `implementation_source_hash`, `resource_status`, `baseline_status`, `nuisance_domain_status`; absence blocks |

Statistics-role files must also match the canonical `statistics_hash`. Every score row must have current v2 target semantics and the same committed statistics, scorer manifest and exact scoring policy. The existing metric inventory validator checks query IDs, regimes, duplicate/missing queries, complete crossing and matching paired targets/provenance. Declared units cannot be inferred from surviving rows. Infinite/undefined primary losses block comparable effects while preserving method/native summaries and errors. Administrative seals prevent accidental peeking, not malicious administrator forgery.

The separate release JSON is `{schema_version:1, mode, manifest_hash, scope_id, rows_sha256, expected_runs_hash, authorization, runs}`. `mode` is `test_only` or `legitimate`; authorization has `authority` (`test_only` or future `task21_unblind_v1`) and `recorded_at`. `expected_runs_hash` is the canonical hash of the frozen run list. Each release run preserves the corresponding `{run_id,method,replicate}`, `status:'completed'` and `rows_hash`, the canonical hash of its entire ordered score-row slice. `rows_sha256` commits the exact unblinded file bytes, which contain a JSON array of existing scorer v2 rows. Failed predictions are completed scored rows with fallback, not missing runs. Unresolved run failure cannot shrink the matrix or unblind. Test-only mode is explicit in both manifest and release; software tests use invented predictions/seeds and cannot consume scientific protected seeds.

Validate release, complete successful run records and file hashes before reading score contents. Scientific mode additionally requires source-bound implementation acceptance, scoped calibration and resource/baseline/nuisance-domain acceptance before score parsing. Successful parsing alone leaves implementation `not_run`; it is not a source review or correctness proof. Task21 must produce and guard the real authorization/seal; this module does not authorize or execute unblinding. It will reuse this boundary rather than invent another trainer/evaluator. Fresh source archives required by Task21 remain that task's responsibility; current exact source checks are unchanged.

Outputs retain source/input hashes, raw paired per-history and per-unit effects, actual unit/history/replicate order, all draws/stages/seeds, all original score rows on successful analysis, normalized/native method summaries, nominal/guarded results and orthogonal gate statuses. Input failures produce `status:'blocked'`, explicit exception type/message and closed permissions; original inputs remain intact. A previously existing output file is rejected rather than overwritten. Software-only analysis returns `analyzed_test_only` and never a scientific success.

## Calibration and design gates: still unfilled

`unfilled_calibration_policy()` returns version 1 with explicit null `scope_id`, `source_hash`, `coverage_floor`, `type1_ceiling`, `fwer_ceiling`, `power_floor`, `outer_n`, `outer_error_budget`, `domain`, `required_cells`, `selection_stream`, `validation_stream`, `nuisance_authorization`. **No production tolerances, outer N, domain, error budget or stream selection have been chosen.**

`evaluate_gate(evidence, frozen_policy)` separates `implementation`, `calibration`, `calibration_cells`, `endpoint_mc`, `secondary_p_mc`, `eligibility`, scoped policy hash, reasons and permissions. Each required cell specifies `{cell_id, metric, fixture_hash, allocation}`; metrics must collectively include primary coverage, primary type I, secondary Holm FWER and power. Evidence retains integer `successes`, exact predeclared `trials`, matching cell/scope/source/policy/fixture/allocation and `rng_stream` equal to the distinct fresh-validation stream. Recompute Clopper–Pearson bounds spending outer budget equally across required cells/tails; never trust an input `qualified` label. Lower-bound requirements qualify if L≥floor, reject if U<floor; upper-bound requirements reverse inequalities; otherwise unresolved. Missing, partial, altered or mismatched evidence cannot qualify. Every outer dataset, including invalid/capped outcomes under the prospective failure policy, must be retained in denominators by the future simulation controller.

All required cells must qualify, implementation must pass, relevant endpoints must be `met/degenerate_zero`, p precision must be adequate, and resource, comparator and nuisance-domain evidence must pass before `confirmation_allowed`. Even code-correct, MC-precise results stay blocked when calibration is unknown/rejected. `nuisance_only_allowed` additionally needs a separate explicit authorized scope/source/allocation, positive time/byte limits and recorded authority; no allocation is supplied here. There is no universal sufficient seed count. Finite seeds/clusters, independence assumptions, population mismatch and conservative calibration uncertainty remain material limitations.
