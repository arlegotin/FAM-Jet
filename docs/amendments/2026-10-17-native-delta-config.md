Historical writes never receive eventual queries. Only fully observed shared-normalization events and absent/unit external gates are supported. Empty/all-gap histories retain safe zero temporal placeholders and return unsupported. Raw per-event decay does not decay S during query delays or gap markers. First event observed gap is zero. No continuous-gap extension is entered.

`t_ref` is fitted from positive training stream gaps before causal encoding, frozen during optimization, and included in delta writer identity. Candidate feature/target statistics and provenance are validated under that reference; a rejected fit restores prior t_ref and leaves prior statistics intact. Writer identity includes E, q/k/v projections, k/v convolutions, decay/erase/write weights, clock/schema policy, and t_ref. Read-only R, q convolution, RMSNorm, output gate/projection and common reader weights affect full inference identity and may reuse a snapshot. Changing a writer component rejects it.
