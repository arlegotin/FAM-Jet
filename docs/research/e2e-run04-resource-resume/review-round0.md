## Blocking finding

**Original global deadline can be exceeded on a successful child exit.** In `e2e-run04-resume-witness.py` lines 125–127, the helper clamps `work_deadline` to the remaining original global allowance. Lines 137–149 check that deadline only while the child is still running. If the child exits just after global expiry and before the next poll, the loop ends without setting a guard. The final success condition at line 170 checks `end < deadline`, where `deadline` is the newly allocated wrapper deadline, and never checks the original global deadline. It can therefore report success after the original global clock expires. This misses the required hard global clock gate. Retain an absolute original global deadline in the receipt and require the final end time to precede it, including the child-exit path. The 20 fake repository fixtures do not cover an exit just after global expiry; `expired_global` covers only prelaunch rejection.
