## Decision and alternatives

Finish a **narrow, independently qualified fixed-writer PoC**, including a fitted convex reader, synthetic protected evaluation, measured costs, both Beijing transfer modes, and artifact-generated reporting. Keep broad learned-writer/all-family competitiveness and optional probing/language unentered. Explicit dated Root amendments must replace open-ended continuation with this finite scope and name every changed estimand, package boundary, numerical domain, selection and stopping policy.

Three paths exist:

1. Continue the six MLP procedures. Their failed recoverable-fixture gate and unresolved optimization make delivery cost unpredictable. A gradient audit might inform later research but is not a prerequisite for a different reader.
2. Freeze panel04's mathematical reader alone. Cheapest legitimate fixed-decoder comparison, but it does not deliver trained-reader adequacy or the full minimum core. Its existing global extreme-cancellation failure also remains.
3. **Choose a separately qualified query-interaction ridge reader**, retaining mathematical projection as a reference. Convex fitting supplies an auditable optimum for its declared finite feature class, avoids indefinite optimizer repair, and permits trainable shape-matched transfer. It establishes neither an optimal decoder nor a memory-capacity limit. Qualification can still fail; report that failure without promoting panel04 into full completion.

Ridge must be supervised fitting on training examples, not the existing projection's identically named regularization. Use deterministic, public query interactions with contracted state, and a finite query basis fixed before qualification. A plain additive linear function of state and query cannot generally route key/window information. Include raw amplitude and explicit support features; fit standardization on training only. A generic contracted reader qualifies minimum generic access; the failed full-state MLP and H2 attribution remain unresolved.
