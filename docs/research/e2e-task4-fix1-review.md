The two filesystem calls are not atomic. FIX1 now invokes this code concurrently with stage execution through `famjet_poc/resources.py:113–115` and the initial/cooperative samples. E4 writes temporary files and renames them at `pipeline.py:22–27`; the existing E3 writer does the same at `famjet_poc/study.py:39–47`.

The guard then refuses completion even though no allocation was exceeded. A protected/test method hit by this race becomes a failed, nonretryable stage after protected access. This is introduced by changing the observer from synchronous boundary inspection to concurrent background sampling; it does not require a hostile file edit or source change.
