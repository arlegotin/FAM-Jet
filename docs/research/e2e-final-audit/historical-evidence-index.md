## Review limits and preserved failures

- The final branch review should use the complete source diff from original base `78f7ba8`, the source/evidence inventories, the run06 execution index, and focused reports. The branch includes 26,405 files and about 2.1 million inserted lines, mostly immutable raw evidence and historical tracked symlinks; a risk-based review should validate manifests and source scopes without manually opening every raw row.
- Confirm the preserved unchanged facts from the run06 source inventory and prior acceptance records: numerical model/data inputs, candidate grids, targets, statistical calibration, E3 math/worker contract, dependency locks, and preregistered thresholds were not changed by report-only recovery. The measured-execution report separately records zero-byte diffs for its named preserved core/E1/E2/E3/transfer inputs.
- Keep the old failed MLP, gradient, extreme-cancellation, binding, and inference calibration-attempt-1 outcomes as failures. Do not convert earlier failures, interrupted runs, or public probes into passes or silently replace/reselect their inputs.
- Scope the handoff to the executed fixed-writer PoC and its recorded evidence. The records do not establish the broader learned-writer or dense-key claims, a HiPPO-delta result, or agent-wide success. Historical Task18/other family work is not evidence for those claims.
- This evidence index deliberately does not open private old04 score files, large run06 inference-history lists, or rerun any experiment/environment command.
