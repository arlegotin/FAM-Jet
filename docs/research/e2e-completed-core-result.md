# Completed fixed-writer PoC — 25 October 2026

The [full report](../../reports/famjet-poc/final.md) and [machine report](../../reports/famjet-poc/final.json) reproduce the sealed numeric experiment. **A useful Jet advantage was not demonstrated.** Confirmation and independent replication each contain 3,072 histories with 256 histories in each of 12 strata. Their Bank-minus-Jet normalized-loss estimates are +0.03535 (95% interval −0.01971 to +0.09042) and +0.03968 (−0.01275 to +0.09211). Both point estimates favor Jet; both intervals include zero. These are conditional on the actual frozen fits and finite selection procedure, excluding retraining variation.

Simple temporal buckets had lower average synthetic loss than either selected learned reader. On the equally tuned real-training arm, the Bank's sensor RMSE was 21.36 µg/m³ versus 68.64 for Jet; their MAEs were 12.71 and 15.35. Shape-matched frozen synthetic transfer favored Jet over Bank, but both performed worse than the simple sensor summaries. Both real modes use the same 56 chronological histories; their 28-/56-day resampling ranges describe this finite period rather than future-period population significance.

The entered comparison uses fixed one-hot addressing, FP64 Jet4 versus Bank8, query-conditioned ridge readers, and one persistent-state budget: 9,345 B/history for K16/V8 and 1,665 B for K12/V1. It does not establish learned-writer/dense-key addressing, arbitrary-reader optimality, all-family competitiveness, language/agent utility, a new representation category, or a multi-budget frontier. Exact arithmetic and all eight entered methods remain visible in the report. Gamma/Laguerre latency is unmeasured; strict prediction-equivalence failures are retained.
